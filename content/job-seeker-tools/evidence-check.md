## Practice, Not an Assessment

All situations and statements are fictional editorial exercises. The explanations concern only the supplied facts, not a real person's qualifications or a real employer's requirements. Two minutes is a suggested window for one situation, not a timer. There is no score or readiness result. Never present a fictional project as your experience.

For real applications, verify the full current posting and follow the actual employer's instructions. A missing detail is not proof that a requirement or feature exists, and it is not proof that it never exists elsewhere.
