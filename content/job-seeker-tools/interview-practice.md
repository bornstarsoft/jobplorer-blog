## A Short Practice, Not a Script

These six prompts and all their examples are fictional Jobplorer exercises, not actual employer questions or a scoring rubric. Five minutes is a suggested practice window, not a deadline. Think through a response or use your own private notes. Never present a fictional example as your experience.

For a real interview, use truthful examples you can safely discuss and follow the actual invitation. The National Careers Service describes STAR as a way to organize an example and recommends preparing for follow-up questions. It is one possible structure, not a requirement for every answer. [National Careers Service: STAR method](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/interview-advice/the-star-method), checked October 23, 2026. This is general preparation context from an England-based public resource, not hiring advice for every country.
