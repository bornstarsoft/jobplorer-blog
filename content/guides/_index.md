# Guides

Choose the question closest to your next step. Each guide connects to resources you can check directly.

[Find guides by your next step, role, and country](/job-seeker-tools/guide-finder/) or [compare job sites](/job-sites/).

## Find Your Next Action

For a short practice session, start with [This Week's Job Search Practice](/job-seeker-tools/weekly-practice/): four fictional exercises with worked responses and a downloadable text pack.

- **My search results describe the wrong work:** [Refine Your Job Search Terms](/guides/refine-your-job-search-terms/) compares candidate phrases, filters, and fictional descriptions without treating titles as guarantees.
- **I want to understand an employer:** [Research an Employer Before Applying](/guides/research-an-employer/) separates company statements, reported experiences, and questions to verify.
- **I want to learn what a role is like:** [Plan an Informational Conversation](/guides/plan-an-informational-conversation/) provides a respectful request, work-focused questions, and a way to keep one person's experience in context.
- **The posting is hard to interpret:** [Read a Job Posting](/guides/read-a-job-posting/) separates the work, stated criteria, and questions still to ask.
- **I am unsure what to practise next:** [Choose What to Learn Next](/guides/choose-what-to-learn-next/) turns a skill goal into a small task, with fictional records and a worked check.
- **I need to explain a project:** [Build a Project Case Study](/guides/build-a-project-case-study/) helps describe your contribution and evidence without inventing results.
- **The application asks for a letter:** [Write a Focused Cover Letter](/guides/write-a-focused-cover-letter/) connects one responsibility to a truthful example, with an annotated fictional letter.
- **An unexpected recruiter contacted me:** [Check Job Offers and Recruiters](/guides/check-job-offers-and-recruiters/) helps separate verified details from warning signs.
- **I have an interview invitation:** [Prepare for an Interview](/guides/prepare-for-an-interview/) connects truthful examples, employer questions, and a video setup check.
- **I have been sent a task brief:** [Prepare for a Work Sample Task](/guides/prepare-for-a-work-sample-task/) separates supplied facts, requested output, and questions, with a fictional exercise and worked response.
- **The interview is over and I am waiting:** [Follow Up After an Interview](/guides/follow-up-after-an-interview/) separates a thank-you note, a status question, and a useful reflection.
- **I am starting my search:** [Entry-Level Job Search](/posts/the-struggles-of-young-job-seekers/) covers reading postings and comparing starting points.
- **I want to review my application:** [Resume Blockers](/posts/why-87-percent-of-job-seekers-abandon-their-resumes/) helps you check materials before submitting.
- **I am unsure about AI in recruitment:** [AI in Recruitment](/posts/the-rise-of-ai-in-recruitment/) explains questions to ask about tools and application processes.
- **I want to use AI without inventing experience:** [Use AI for Job Search Preparation](/guides/use-ai-for-job-search-preparation/) provides fictional practice inputs, prompts, and an output-checking example.
- **I am researching AI-related game work:** [AI-Assisted Game Development Roles](/posts/ai-creators-future-game-development/) offers role-research starting points.

## Put the Reading to Work

Use the [job seeker worksheets](/job-seeker-tools/) to record a shortlist, prepare questions, and review progress each week. For a more specific starting point, browse [career guides](/career-guides/) or [country guides](/country-guides/).

Entry-Level Job Search, Resume Blockers, AI in Recruitment, and AI-Assisted Game Development Roles retain their original archive URLs. The wider [legacy news archive](/posts/) includes older material and should be read in its historical context. Confirm current facts with official sources.
