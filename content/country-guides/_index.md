# Country Guides

Country guides help job seekers understand what to research before comparing work options in another country.

Topics may include common job search channels, official public employment resources, language considerations, local hiring customs, and documents to verify through official sources.

## Reviewed Country Guides

- [Job Search in South Korea: Where to Start](/country-guides/south-korea/) - Review cautious starting points for public employment resources, job sites, employer pages, and language considerations.
- [Job Search in Canada: Where to Start](/country-guides/canada/) - Explore official public employment resources, job search channels, and employer information to verify directly.
- [Job Search in the United States: Where to Start](/country-guides/united-states/) - Review official public employment resources, job search channels, state workforce starting points, and employer information to verify directly.

Additional guides will be added gradually after official-source review. They may help users identify job search starting points, public employment resources, and job-site categories to verify directly.

Jobplorer does not provide visa advice, immigration advice, legal advice, tax advice, labor advice, relocation advice, or guarantees of eligibility, employment, salary outcomes, or hiring outcomes. Always check official government, employer, and job-site sources for current requirements and details.
