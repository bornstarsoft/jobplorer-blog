## Choose an Official Starting Point

[Canada.ca's Jobs page](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs.html) links to Job Bank, government jobs, and other employment resources. Use that directory to choose the resource that fits your research. [Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/) is a linked job-search starting point; check its current pages directly for available functions and access details.

## Make a Regional Shortlist

1. Choose a city or region and a role title to investigate.
2. Record the posting's original language and any language expectations it explicitly states. Do not infer them from a portal's English or French interface.
3. Compare the employer name, job reference, location, and application destination.
4. Use the official resource's own directory when looking for local employment services; check the service's current scope before relying on it.
5. Record the date checked and unanswered questions in your [application worksheet](/job-seeker-tools/).

## Compare Additional Channels

Use [LinkedIn](/job-sites/sites/linkedin/) and [Indeed](/job-sites/sites/indeed/) as additional research channels. For remote postings, apply the [work-arrangement checklist](/career-guides/remote-workers/) rather than assuming a country-wide search defines where work can be done.

## Scope and Limitations

Review note: this country guide has had an official-source review pass, but public resources, job-site availability, employer requirements, and application processes can change.

This reviewed guide may be useful for planning job search research in Canada. It can be a starting point for comparing broad search channels such as general job sites, professional networking platforms, company career pages, and official public employment resources.

Official public resources such as Job Bank, Canada.ca job search pages, and employment centre resources can help readers identify employment-service starting points. Job seekers should still check each official resource, employer career page, and job posting directly for current details.

Job site availability, language support, account access, employer requirements, job postings, application processes, policies, and features can change.
