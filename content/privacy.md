# Privacy Policy

Jobplorer is currently a static informational website. It provides job site research starting points, country guides, career guides, remote work resources, job seeker tools, and legacy archive content.

Jobplorer currently does not provide user accounts, login features, direct job applications, payment processing, comments, or account dashboards.

## Basic Site Analytics

Jobplorer may use basic site analytics, such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or similar privacy-conscious site analytics, to understand site performance, popular pages, traffic patterns, and technical issues.

Analytics information may include general usage data such as page views, referrers, device or browser information, approximate region, and performance signals. This information is used to improve the website and understand how pages are used.

## Guide Finder and Job Site Comparison

The Guide Finder offers fixed choices for a research goal, role topic, and country resource. It does not ask for your name, contact details, resume, application records, or free-text answers. Its code filters links on the page without submitting or saving these selections, and it does not place them in the page URL.

Jobplorer's GA4 tag is excluded from the Guide Finder and Job Site Comparison pages. Other pages retain their analytics configuration. Hosting services may still receive ordinary page requests, and linked external sites have their own policies. These statements describe the two pages' implementation, not a claim of zero data processing across the whole site.

## Weekly Job Search Practice

Weekly practice contains fictional exercises and worked responses. It has no answer fields, account, file upload, or saved progress. Expanding a response does not submit an answer or store a selection. The optional text download contains public exercises, not your responses; your browser manages the downloaded file on your device.

The optional calendar export processes a selected first practice date in the current browser tab to create four all-day events. The date is not sent to Jobplorer or placed in cookies, browser storage, or URL parameters. The calendar file is generated locally; Jobplorer does not connect to a calendar account, send invitations, or deliver reminders. If you import the file, your chosen calendar app or provider may store and sync the events under its own settings and policy. Avoid importing the same plan repeatedly because calendar apps may create duplicates.

Jobplorer's GA4 tag is excluded from this page. Ordinary hosting requests and any hosting-level analytics are separate from the exercise controls. Other Jobplorer pages and external destinations retain their own analytics and privacy behavior. This is not a claim that the entire site processes no data.

## Interview Practice

Interview practice contains six public fictional prompts and examples. It has no answer input, microphone access, recording, account, upload, scoring or saved progress. Topic selection and previous/next controls run in the current tab without sending selections or storing them in cookies or browser storage. Question links use public fragment identifiers only; your browser may retain navigation history or bookmarks.

The optional text download contains public prompts and examples, not your answers. Jobplorer's GA4 tag is excluded from the page. Hosting analytics and security services may still process ordinary requests and set security cookies; linked guides may use their existing analytics. This is a description of the exercise implementation, not a whole-site zero-data-processing claim.

## Contact Information

If you contact the site owner, information you choose to provide may be used to respond to the inquiry, review corrections, evaluate feedback, or handle related communication.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information, job applications, payment details, passwords, government identification numbers, or confidential employer information through general contact channels.

## External Links

Jobplorer may link to external job sites, employer pages, public resources, and other third-party websites. External websites have their own privacy policies, terms, cookies, analytics, account systems, payment systems, and application processes.

Review each external website's own policies before using it or submitting information.

## Changes

Jobplorer may update this Privacy Policy as the site changes. Check this page for the current version.
